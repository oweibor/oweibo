### Commercial Opportunities This Unlocks for You

This single feature dramatically increases monetization potential:

* **Enterprise tier** : “Dedicated AI Teammate” — tenants pay extra for their own branded bot + priority queue + dedicated warm pool.
* **Agency / SaaS resale** : Agencies can white-label Oweibo and give each of *their* clients a custom bot.
* **Usage-based upsell** : Charge per 1,000 messages or per active chat session.
* **Marketplace** : Sell pre-built “skills” or domain agents that tenants can activate in their chat gateway.
* **Sticky product** : Once users chat with the agent daily, they are far less likely to churn.
